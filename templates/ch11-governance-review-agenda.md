## Agenda

1. Reconfirm the declared population, exclusions, assistance boundary, and
   prohibited authority.
2. Review unresolved, expired, unsatisfied, and unverified required gates
   before reviewing achievements.
3. Inspect direct evaluation results, missingness, uncertainty, corrections,
   adverse cases, and indeterminate outcomes.
4. Review source health, access denials, injection tests, privacy boundaries,
   reliability holds, and economic-budget exhaustion.
5. Confirm human command, positive acknowledgement where applicable, manual
   fallback, stop ownership, and the conditions that force demotion.
6. Review transferred work, operating cost, and the non-AI alternative. Cost
   control is not evidence of operational value.
7. Record dissent and choose continue, narrow, hold, or stop. Name the evidence,
   owner, reviewer, expiry, next action, and re-entry condition for every gap.
